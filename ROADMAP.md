# camp roadmap — closed

camp is **retired**. There is no further roadmap.

Its purpose — free, tip-fresh, decoded Arbitrum One data — is now served by
**nuthatch**: https://nuthatch-indexer.com/

Phases 1, A–I shipped over the project's life (lookups, decoded protocol tables,
`/explore` dashboards, raw SQL, streaming, OpenAPI, anonymous tokens, a
self-built engine, full instrumentation, the Postgres wire protocol, and an MCP
server). The full history is in git. Nothing more is planned here.
